Zap tracks price drops across India's major shopping sites and brings them into one feed, so you can see what got cheaper today without opening five apps.

WHAT ZAP DOES
Every day, thousands of discounts appear across Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Ajio and other retailers. Zap scans them, filters out the noise, and shows you a short, readable list of what's actually worth a look.

ONE FEED, MANY STORES
Deals from multiple retailers sit side by side, with the current price, the discount percentage and the product rating shown up front. Judge an offer in a second, then tap through to the retailer to buy.

WHAT YOU'LL FIND
- Price drops on electronics, fashion, home, kitchen, beauty and more
- Limited-time and lightning offers
- Coupon codes and bank offers where they apply
- Product ratings next to every deal, so price isn't the only thing you're judging

PRICE-DROP ALERTS
Turn on notifications and Zap sends you a small set of the day's larger price drops. A few a day, not a stream.

BUILT TO BE QUICK
- No sign-up, no account, no personal details
- Opens straight to the feed
- Every deal links directly to the retailer's own product page

Zap is a discovery tool, not a shop. You buy on Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa or Ajio as usual, with their pricing, payment and returns.
